References

Ryoo, J.-M., C. Liu, D. W. Waugh, R. Ueyama, and J. R. Spackman, 2025: Examining the linkages between GPM satellite-derived precipitation features associated with atmospheric rivers and Rossby wave breaking. Geophys. Res. Lett., 52, e2024GL112943. doi:10.1029/2024GL112943

The 2025 study classifies ARs by wave-breaking type, not ENSO phase; it is linked to La Niña / El Niño through the 2013 finding that La Niña favors anticyclonic and El Niño favors cyclonic breaking. The 2026–27 implication is an inference from these results.
